# Multi-Site Clinical Workstream Rollout | Generic Example

**Type:** Template derived from professional themes, not an actual employer record.

## Objective

Coordinate service timelines, required documentation, clinical consultations and staff implementation across a local clinical team and a remote partner site.

## Workstreams

1. Governance and scope: program charter, escalation and approvals.
2. Timeline: service deadlines, review cadence, and assignment ownership.
3. Evidence and data quality: definitions, QA and version control.
4. Staff enablement: orientation, competency sign-off and follow-up.
5. Monitoring: weekly delivery status, blockers, risk and actions.
6. Handoff: ongoing operational ownership, records retention and audits.

## Collaboration design

- **Asana-style tracker:** task, workstream, owner, due date, status, dependency, risk, evidence link, completion date.
- **Slack-style updates:** agreed channels, no protected records, links to controlled repositories rather than copied confidential data.
- **Governance:** project owner coordinates; clinical and administrative approvals remain with authorized decision makers.

## Measures

Overdue milestones, unresolved actions, owner assignment completeness, time to consultation follow-up, and demonstrated adoption. Avoid presenting correlation from unverified percentages as established implementation effectiveness.
