# Classroom Sensory Regulation Environment | Proposal-Only Charter

**Status: Proposed, not installed or evaluated.**

**Problem:** A high-stimulation classroom serving approximately ages 5–9 needs a safer, supervised regulation area with attention to foreseeable head impacts, hitting, and drop-to-floor behaviors.

**Constraints:** Funding ceiling **$1,000** and 14 calendar days **after authorization** as a proposed delivery target. Nonpermanent installation; no removal or repurposing of essential existing equipment. Subject to procurement, safety and administrative approval.

## Scope and stage gates

1. Obtain approvals and define staff responsibilities.
2. Conduct environmental hazard review (tipping, entrapment, foreseeable impacts, supervision, hygiene, accessibility).
3. Obtain quotations and verify life-cycle cost.
4. Conduct procurement and nonpermanent installation only when authorized.
5. Document inspection, orientation and maintenance responsibility.
6. Close and hand off project after acceptance.

## Proposed measures

Spend vs authorized budget, milestone completion, inspection pass, documented staff orientation, assigned maintenance owner. **No claim of self-injury or incident reduction.** Such an outcome would require appropriately authorized prospective measurement and careful interpretation.
