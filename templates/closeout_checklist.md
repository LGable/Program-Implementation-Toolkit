# Project Closeout & Ownership Checklist

- [ ] Objective met or formally withdrawn; evidence links verified.
- [ ] Clinical/admin review obtained where necessary.
- [ ] Risks closed, accepted or delegated with owner and escalation path.
- [ ] Standard work, training and job aids filed in approved systems.
- [ ] Quality measures and review cadence assigned.
- [ ] Cost/benefit claims labeled observed, estimated, simulated or unmeasured.
- [ ] Final administrative sign-off retained by proper authority.
- [ ] Confidential source material retained only in authorized storage.
- [ ] Project status changed from active initiative to routine operation, if appropriate.
