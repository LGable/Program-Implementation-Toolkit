# Implementation Follow-Through | Generic Communications SOP

1. Record a recommendation with a neutral task ID; no personal health or education details in an open channel.
2. Assign one accountable owner and due date in the approved project tracker.
3. State readiness criteria: materials, staff competency, sign-off and dependencies.
4. Follow up at agreed cadence, separating 'message sent', 'acknowledged' and 'implemented'.
5. Require evidence of execution appropriate to the organizational policy, not just a checked box.
6. Escalate blockers to the authorized supervisor; do not assume the communications platform is the system of record.
7. At closeout document implementation status, owner, date and outstanding risks.

**Measurement note:** I observed preliminary improvement in post-consult implementation at Merakey. A numerical percentage is not published here because the available materials do not specify the denominator, period, or whether the change was relative or percentage points.
