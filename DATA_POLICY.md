# Data and attribution rules

- All example CSV data in this repository are **synthetic demonstrations**, not patient, employee, student, payer, or employer records.
- Real work described in a career case study is **self-reported historical experience**, not independently audited performance.
- An illustrative financial model is not an actual ledger, audited revenue, realized profit, or authorized budget.
- Do not add employer exports, protected health information, student information, private financials, credentials, or internal communications.
- I exclude employer-restricted material and seek appropriate permission where publication rights are required.
- Some template documentation was assembled with freelancer support and AI assistance; it should not be represented as material written unaided by me.
- No license to third parties is granted by default; content ownership and contributions should be reviewed before reuse.
